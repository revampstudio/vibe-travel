The accompanying world-land.json contains projected land outlines derived from Natural Earth 1:110m physical land data (ne_110m_land.geojson).

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Downloaded 17 October 2026. The source coordinates were transformed to a 720 × 360 equirectangular map, rounded to one decimal place, and stored as SVG path data.
